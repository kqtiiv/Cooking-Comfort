# Cooking Comfort

What could be better than a nice warm meal with some extra thought to it! something special ;)
Welcome your customers and lend a helpful ear. Maybe you could make their day! Or... ruin it.

Prepare a perfect dish just for them.

Controls: 
WASD move 
Right Click and drag for camera movement 
E to interact (in front of items) 
Left click to progress dialogue :) 

Oh and of course it's super cute!!
